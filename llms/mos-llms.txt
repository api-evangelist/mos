## About

Mos was founded in 2017 by Amira Yahyaoui and is backed by Sequoia Capital, Lux Capital and Expa.
It started as a financial-aid application service (FAFSA filing plus human advisor support), added
a fee-free student checking account and debit card in 2021, then shut the banking product down in
2023 and refocused on financial aid. The product today matches students against a scholarship
database Mos describes as $160 billion in available funding, and adds college financial-planning
tools and access to a personal advisor. Sequoia's company page records Mos as acquired in 2025;
the acquirer is not disclosed.
